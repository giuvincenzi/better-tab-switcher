Looking for a tab among many? Search for it in the switcher.

Search a tab

  1. Open the switcher and click the Search tabs field in its toolbar. The switcher now stays open, even when you let go of Alt.
  2. Type a few letters. The switcher shows only the tabs whose title or address contains them. Upper and lower case don't matter, and the part of the title that matches is highlighted.
  3. Press Enter to open the first tab found. Or press ↓ to move into the tabs found, then Enter to open the selected one. A click on a tab opens it too.

When no tab matches, the switcher says “No tabs match your search.” Change what you typed, or press Esc to close the switcher.

The search looks only at the tabs the switcher shows: those of the current window, or of all windows with Show tabs from set to All windows.

The search field is in the toolbar. If you turned off Show toolbar, turn it back on to search.
